Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy describes how personal data collected through thesyum.com is processed, under EU Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR) and applicable law.

1. Data controller

The data controller is the individual behind the Thesyum project, reachable at info@thesyum.com for any question regarding personal data.

Note: Thesyum is a project developed by an individual. Full identifying details of the controller are provided on request by writing to the address above.

2. What data we collect

Through the contact form on the site, we collect exclusively the data you choose to provide us with:

  • Full name — so we can reply to you personally
  • Email address — to get back to you about your request
  • Phone number (optional) — only if you choose to enter it
  • Message — whatever you write in the form

We don't collect data covertly and we don't purchase contact lists from third parties.

3. Why we process your data

Data submitted through the contact form is used for one single purpose: replying to your request and, if you requested a demo, arranging the follow-up. It is not used for automated marketing or newsletter sign-ups without your explicit consent.

The legal basis for this processing is your consent and the performance of pre-contractual steps taken at your request (Art. 6 GDPR).

4. How the form data is handled

The contact form is managed through the Formspree service, which receives and forwards submitted messages. Data therefore passes through this provider's servers, which acts as a data processor. We invite you to also review Formspree's privacy policy for details on their processing.

5. How long we keep the data

We retain contact data for as long as needed to handle your request, and for a reasonable period afterward for any related follow-up. After that period, or upon your request for deletion, the data is erased.

6. Who we share data with

Your data is never sold or transferred to third parties for commercial purposes. It may be processed solely by the technical providers that make the site and contact form work (such as hosting and Formspree), strictly to the extent necessary.

7. Your rights

As a data subject, you have the right to:

  • access your data and obtain a copy of it
  • request correction if inaccurate
  • request deletion ("right to be forgotten")
  • restrict or object to processing
  • withdraw consent at any time
  • file a complaint with the competent data protection authority

To exercise these rights, simply write to info@thesyum.com.

8. Cookies

thesyum.com uses only technical cookies necessary for it to function, and does not use profiling cookies for advertising purposes. Any embedded external services (such as web fonts) may set their own technical cookies. No advertising profiling of users is carried out.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated over time. The version in force is always the one published on this page, with the last-updated date shown above.

For any question about how we handle your data, write to info@thesyum.com. We reply within a few business days.
